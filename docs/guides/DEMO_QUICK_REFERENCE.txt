SHAP EXPLANATION READING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Red bar (negative value)  = pushes score toward ANOMALOUS
  Green bar (positive)      = pushes score toward NORMAL

  Example: High SYN count + odd packet rate + low bytes = Anomalous
           Interpretation: Likely port scan or DDoS SYN flood setup

ML PIPELINE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Raw Features (12 flow metrics)
         ↓
  StandardScaler (normalize)
         ↓
  Isolation Forest (200 trees, detect anomalies)
         ↓
  DDoS Rule Engine (SYN/port scan/volumetric checks)
         ↓
  Combined Score + Attack Type Label

PERFORMANCE (CTU-13)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Precision: 0.695    (when we flag something, 70% are real attacks)
  Recall:    0.555    (we catch 55% of actual attacks)
  ROC-AUC:   0.706    (good separation between attack & normal)

ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q: "Why such low recall?"
A: "We prioritize catching rare attacks over speed. Missing 1 real attack is
   worse than investigating 10 false alarms."

Q: "Does this require labels?"
A: "No. Isolation Forest is unsupervised. The rule engine uses hand-tuned
   thresholds based on domain knowledge."

Q: "Can I integrate with my SIEM?"
A: "Yes. Export alerts as CSV/JSON, import to Splunk/ELK."

Q: "What about false positives on my network?"
A: "Retrain on your baseline (Model Training tab) to adapt to your traffic."

Q: "Does this work offline?"
A: "Yes. All detection runs locally. No cloud calls."

TROUBLESHOOTING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
